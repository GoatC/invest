# 5. Phone redesign 手機改版

**Phone redesign 手機改版** (decided by Hugo on 2026-11-10, after a check of all 28 pages at iPhone width: 43 of 54 tables scrolled sideways, the listed holdings showed no figure without sliding, 307 of 1,054 things to tap were under 40px). Built in steps, each its own pull request. Hugo 於 2026-11-10 決定（以 iPhone 寬度檢查全部 28 頁後：54 張表中 43 張需橫向捲動、上市持股不滑動看不到任何數字、1,054 個可點項目中 307 個小於 40px）。分步建置，每步一個 pull request。

1. Speed 速度 (done 完成, #37): ECharts in a chunk of its own, pages seen in the last five minutes open from memory. A full-page loading skeleton and streamed page parts measured slower and were left out. 圖表函式庫獨立載入、看過的頁面保留 5 分鐘；全頁骨架與分區串流量測較慢，未採用。
2. Phone layout base 手機版面基礎 (done 完成): under 760px a name shows Chinese alone, tiles sit two to a row, a row of choices stays on one line and slides sideways, a table of more than three columns shows as a card per row (the first cell its title, each other cell under its column's name), and nothing to tap is smaller than 44px. 760px 以下名稱只顯示中文、數字卡兩欄、篩選列單行可橫滑、超過三欄的表格改為每列一張卡（第一格為標題，其餘各格在欄名下方）、可點項目不小於 44px。
3. Holdings overview 持倉總覽 (done 完成; app/holdings/overview.tsx, lib/models/holding-view.ts, components/phone-tabs.tsx; the views are picked with `view` in the address, the closes drawn as SVG on the server; a wide screen still shows every part of the page at once, with the holdings table under the cards 檢視以網址的 `view` 切換，走勢由伺服器畫成 SVG；寬螢幕仍一次顯示全部區塊，卡片下方保留持股表格): sub-tabs 總覽｜交易｜現金流｜觀察清單. The overview opens with four tiles (value, unrealised gain, the last session's move, satellite share) and a card per holding: value and weight, three months of closes with each buy marked and the average cost as a dashed line, the last session's move and the move since cost, and signal chips. Other views to switch to: gain ranking bars, each holding's buys and price against its average cost on one scale (with the 52-week range, and F12's buy and trim zones for a stock), a treemap (area = value, colour = move), and a signal matrix (a row per holding, a column per kind of signal). A "+" button at the lower right opens the new-trade form, which leaves the first screen. 子分頁：總覽｜交易｜現金流｜觀察清單。總覽以四張數字卡（市值、未實現損益、最近交易日漲跌、衛星占比）開頭，接著每檔一張卡：市值與權重、近三個月收盤（標出每筆買進、虛線為平均成本）、最近交易日與累計漲跌、訊號標籤。可切換的其他檢視：損益排行條、以平均成本為中線的買點對現價（含 52 週高低；個股加 F12 買入區與減碼區）、板塊圖（面積為市值、顏色為漲跌）、訊號燈號表。右下角「＋」開啟新增交易，表單不再占第一屏。
4. Allocation and performance 配置與績效 (done 完成; the bars are drawn on the server in app/allocation/page.tsx, the drift chart's table folds under them; `contributions` in lib/models/performance.ts; the per-holding FX split moved to the 逐檔 view, the gain ranking stays on the holdings page 目標區間條由伺服器繪製、原表格收合在下方；外幣持股的匯率拆解移到「逐檔」，損益排行留在持倉頁): each class as a bar against its target and its 5/25 band, with the TWD amount over or under, in place of the drift chart and its table (the donut stays below); Performance gains a "逐檔" view with each holding's contribution to the return in percentage points (they add up to the TWR) and the gain ranking bars. 每個類別一條目標區間條（實際、目標、5/25 容許區間、差多少台幣），取代偏離圖與其表格（圓餅圖保留在下方）；績效新增「逐檔」：各持股對報酬的貢獻（百分點，合計等於 TWR）與損益排行條。
5. Navigation 功能分區 (done 完成; components/site-nav.tsx `SubNav`, components/ticker-tabs.tsx; the holdings tab keeps its own sub-tabs from step 3 and takes in red flags through the watchlist and each stock's tabs 持倉沿用第 3 步的子分頁，紅旗指標經觀察清單與個股分頁進入): the four tabs stay; each gains a row of sub-tabs and takes in the analysis pages that answer its question (addresses unchanged). 儀表板 Dashboard: 本週｜提案｜波動警報, a waiting proposal second on the page, the data's date and the refresh button at the top. 持倉 Holdings: as step 3, the watchlist holding valuation and red flags. 績效 Performance: 整體｜逐檔｜歸因｜費用與稅. 配置 Allocation: 目標｜合併透視｜產業熱圖｜壓力測試 (the efficient frontier beside the stress test). 更多 More: life planning, regression, cost of equity, rules, notifications, data and import. One page per stock, with sub-tabs for its chart, valuation, red flags and β. 四個分頁不變，各加一排子分頁並收進回答同一問題的分析頁（網址不變）；一檔股票一個入口。
6. Charts 圖表 (done 完成, 2026-11-11): on a phone a value read by touch shows along the chart's top edge, clear of the finger; a text inside a chart (a series or axis name, a tooltip line) is in Chinese alone there, as names are (`chartText` in components/echarts.tsx; accessible names keep both languages); the sector heatmap draws three periods on a phone (1 week, 3 months, 1 year; the table under it keeps all five) with each sector's Chinese name on one line; one colour convention for gains and losses across the app, config `sectors.colour`, red up and green down by default (Hugo, 2026-11-10), set once on the page and worn by every figure that is a move, each with its sign or ▲▼, while a status keeps its ✓ ! ✕ icon; a chart takes 0.3 s to draw or to move to new data and nothing moves with reduced motion on. No chart plays by itself, and a figure does not animate when it changes. 手機上以觸控讀值時，數值顯示在圖表上緣，不被手指遮住；圖表內的文字（數列與座標軸名稱、提示列）在手機上只用中文（無障礙名稱仍為中英）；產業熱圖在手機畫三個期間（1 週、3 月、1 年，下方表格保留五個）、產業名稱中文一行；漲跌配色全 App 一致，讀 config `sectors.colour`，預設紅漲綠跌，凡是漲跌的數字都套用並附正負號或 ▲▼，狀態維持 ✓ ! ✕ 圖示；圖表繪製與更新為 0.3 秒，開啟「減少動態效果」時不動。沒有自動播放的圖，數字改變時也不做動畫。
7. App feel 像 App 一樣順 (done 完成, 2026-11-13, asked by Hugo; components/tab-bar.tsx, components/splash.tsx, components/preload.tsx, lib/splash.ts): three parts. 三個部分。
   - Glass tab bar 玻璃分頁列: a capsule that floats over the foot of the page, the page showing through it, blurred live by the browser; the selection is a lens moved by springs. A finger laid on the bar lifts the lens; it follows the finger from tab to tab, stretching with its speed, and the tab it is let go over opens; scrolling down draws the bar in to its icons, scrolling up brings the names back. Every name sits on one line in one size and weight, the current tab differing by colour alone (the dashboard's name had sat a point higher, in a heavier weight). Apple's own Liquid Glass material is given to native apps only: a web page cannot ask iOS for it, so this is built from what a page has (backdrop-filter, pointer events, a spring worked out each frame), not a picture of the look; the style sheet does ask for Apple's material first (`-apple-visual-effect`), should WebKit ever grant it to a page. With "reduce motion" on, nothing springs or shrinks. 浮在頁面底部的膠囊，透出後方頁面並由瀏覽器即時模糊；選取指示是一片由彈簧驅動的鏡片。手指按住分頁列時鏡片浮起，跟著手指在分頁間滑動並依速度拉長，放開時開啟手指下的分頁；往下捲動時分頁列縮成只剩圖示，往上捲動時名稱回來。所有名稱同一基線、同一字級與字重，目前分頁只以顏色區分（原本「儀表板」字樣高了一點且較粗）。Apple 的 Liquid Glass 材質只開放給原生 App，網頁無法向 iOS 取用，因此以網頁可用的能力（backdrop-filter、指標事件、逐格計算的彈簧）實作行為，而不是臨摹外觀；樣式表仍會先要求 Apple 的材質（`-apple-visual-effect`），日後 WebKit 若開放即自動採用。開啟「減少動態效果」時不彈跳、不縮小。
   - Opening screen 啟動畫面: the mark on the poster's colour. An iPhone shows it as the launch image the moment the icon is tapped (public/splash, one file per screen size, light and dark, drawn by `npm run splash-images`; iOS keeps them from the day the app is added to the home screen, so the icon must be added again to get them); the page then arrives with the same screen over it (the layout reads nothing from the database itself, so only the page's own figures are waited for; the status notice and the banner follow), and it stays, with a bar that fills, until the pages one tap away have been fetched (see Preloading) or eight seconds have passed. Once per opening: a reload within the same visit goes straight to the page. The page reaches every edge of the screen (`viewport-fit=cover`, status bar `black-translucent`): a band of the poster's colour stays behind the status bar on every page. 啟動畫面為主色底上的 App 標誌。iPhone 在點下圖示的瞬間以啟動圖顯示（public/splash，每種螢幕尺寸一張、淺色與深色各一，由 `npm run splash-images` 產生；iOS 在加入主畫面當下就存下啟動圖，所以要重新加入主畫面才會出現）；接著頁面送達時最上層就是同一個畫面（版面本身不讀資料庫，只等頁面自己的數字；狀態通知與橫幅隨後送出），畫面停留並顯示進度條，直到「點一下就到」的頁面預載完成（見預載）或滿 8 秒。每次開啟只出現一次，同一次使用中重新整理不再顯示。頁面延伸到螢幕四邊（`viewport-fit=cover`、狀態列 `black-translucent`），每一頁的狀態列後方都有一條主色帶。
   - Preloading 預載: when the app opens it fetches every page the navigation can open, whole (twenty-one pages), and the chart library, so a tap opens the page from the phone's memory with no request to the server. In two stages: the opening screen waits for the pages one tap away (the tab bar's four and the sub-tabs of the tab that opened); the pages two taps away (the other tabs' sub-tabs, those under More) follow behind the page. Waiting for all of them held the opening screen about half as long again. A page's own choices (a view, a pool, a period on Holdings, Performance, Allocation, Sectors and Attribution) are fetched when that page shows. A page fetched this way is kept thirty minutes (next.config.ts `staleTimes.static`); the set is fetched again when a save clears it, when the thirty minutes are up while the app is on screen, and when the app comes back after five minutes or more in the background, which also reloads the page on screen. Pages reached from inside a page (a holding's chart, a form) are still fetched when tapped, and the page on screen stays until they arrive: no page is wrapped in a loading placeholder, which would blank the screen on such a tap. `localStorage.preload = 'off'` turns it off on a device. Measured with tests/e2e/bench.mjs on 2026-11-13 (processor slowed four times, 100 ms a request from the phone, 5 ms to the database): the opening screen shows at 0.3–0.5 s and lifts at about 2.7 s (3.9 s when it waited for every page; 1.2 s with the preloading off); a tab then opens with no request, its title showing in about 0.37 s, of which nearly all is the phone drawing the page. 開啟 App 時先把導覽能開啟的每一頁完整抓好（共 21 頁），連同圖表函式庫，之後點分頁直接從手機記憶體開啟，不再向伺服器要資料。分兩段：啟動畫面只等「點一下就到」的頁面（分頁列的四頁，以及開啟的那個分頁的子分頁）；要點兩下才到的頁面（其他分頁的子分頁、「更多」裡的頁面）在畫面顯示後接著抓。全部等完會讓啟動畫面多停約一半時間。頁面內的選項（持倉、績效、配置、產業熱圖、歸因的檢視、資金池、期間）在該頁顯示時預載。預載的頁面保留 30 分鐘（next.config.ts `staleTimes.static`）；儲存資料後、App 在前景滿 30 分鐘時，以及 App 在背景 5 分鐘以上再回來時（同時重新載入目前頁面）會重新抓一次。從頁面內進入的頁面（個股走勢、表單）仍在點選時才載入，載入期間保留原畫面：頁面不包載入佔位，否則這類點選會先變空白。`localStorage.preload = 'off'` 可在單一裝置關閉。2026-11-13 以 tests/e2e/bench.mjs 量測（處理器放慢 4 倍、手機每次請求 100 毫秒、資料庫 5 毫秒）：啟動畫面在 0.3–0.5 秒出現、約 2.7 秒收起（等全部頁面時為 3.9 秒；關閉預載時 1.2 秒）；之後點分頁不發出請求，約 0.37 秒顯示標題，幾乎都是手機繪製頁面的時間。
8. Native shell 原生外殼 (steps 1–3 built 第 1–3 步完成, 2026-11-14, asked by Hugo: Apple's Liquid Glass is given to native apps only, and he wants the system's own tab bar; ios/, lib/shell.ts, components/shell-bridge.tsx, docs/ios-app.md). An iOS app beside the web app, not in place of it: the same site on Vercel, the same Supabase project, no page rewritten and no data moved; the installed web app works as before. Signed with a free Apple ID, so it is built and installed again from Hugo's Mac every 7 days (Apple's limit; also 3 such apps on a phone); he will use it for a while before deciding whether that is worth it. 與網頁版並存的 iOS App：同一個 Vercel 網站、同一個 Supabase 專案，不重寫頁面、不搬資料，PWA 照常可用。以免費 Apple ID 簽署，每 7 天需從 Hugo 的 Mac 重新建置安裝（Apple 的限制；一台手機最多 3 個自簽 App）；先試用再決定是否值得。
   - Shape 架構: the system's tab bar (UITabBarController, drawn by iOS in Liquid Glass, drawing in on scrolling down) over one web view. One web view, not one per tab: a tap on a native tab asks the page to open it (`window.longview.select`), and the page opens from what the preloader keeps, as under the web tab bar; four web views would fetch everything four times and show a save in one tab as stale figures in the other three. 系統分頁列加單一 WebView：點原生分頁時請網頁開啟，頁面由預載的記憶體出來；四個 WebView 會讓預載變四倍，且一頁存檔後其他三頁仍是舊資料。
   - The two sides 兩邊的約定 (lib/shell.ts): the shell marks the page before it is read (`data-shell` on `<html>`) and tells it the room its bar takes (`--shell-bottom`); the page then draws no tab bar of its own and, each time its address or the More list changes, sends the shell where it is: signed in or not, the path, the tab it belongs to, and the five tabs with their names. The tabs, their order and which pages belong to which come from components/nav-pages.ts alone; the shell keeps no list of pages. Signed out, the shell hides its bar. 外殼在頁面載入前標記 `<html data-shell>` 並告知分頁列佔的高度；網頁不再畫自己的分頁列，每次換頁或開關「更多」時回報登入狀態、路徑、所屬分頁與五個分頁的名稱。分頁的名稱、順序與歸屬只由 nav-pages.ts 決定，外殼不另存一份。
   - What stays the page's 留在網頁的: sub-tabs, a page's own choices, and the More list (which fills the screen there as that tab's page, with the refresh button and sign-out). The dashboard sits in the middle of the bar as on the web, not raised: the system's bar has no raised button, and Hugo chose the system's glass over the round button (2026-11-14). 子分頁、頁內選項與「更多」清單留在網頁（在外殼裡「更多」整頁顯示）。儀表板仍在分頁列正中間但不突起：系統分頁列沒有突起的圓鈕，Hugo 選擇系統玻璃而非圓鈕。
   - Signing in 登入: the web view keeps its cookies on the phone, apart from Safari's: signed in once, the session lasts through restarts and through each week's install over the app (deleting the app clears it). 登入一次後，重開與每週覆蓋安裝都保留；刪除 App 才會清掉。
   - Opening, status bar, edges 啟動、狀態列、邊緣: the launch screen is the same mark on the same colour, held until the page has drawn its own opening screen, which then shows the progress as before; the page still runs under the status bar and keeps its band there (`--safe-top` works unchanged), and the shell sets the status bar's text white. Nothing of this is read outside the shell. 原生啟動畫面與網頁啟動畫面同色同標誌，接成一個畫面；`--safe-top` 照舊，狀態列文字由外殼設為白色。PWA 不讀取任何外殼設定。
   - Pushes 推播: none reach the shell. A web view is given no Web Push (Apple's own answer on its developer forum), and Apple's push service needs the paid programme. Hugo's choice (2026-11-14): the installed web app stays on the home screen and receives every push as now; the shell schedules notifications on the phone itself for what can be known ahead (step 3, below). The notifications page says so inside the shell. 外殼收不到推播：WebView 沒有 Web Push，APNs 需付費帳號。Hugo 的決定：保留主畫面的 PWA 照常收推播；可預知的提醒（配息日、事件前、每週檢視、7 天到期）由外殼排本機通知（第 3 步）。
   - Checked without a Mac 沒有 Mac 時的檢查: the shell is written in a cloud session with no Xcode. GitHub Actions builds it with Xcode 26 and Xcode 27 on every change (.github/workflows/ios.yml, unsigned, for the simulator); the page's side is covered by unit tests (lib/shell.test.ts) and by the end-to-end check, which stands in for the shell. Signing, the install and the look of the glass can only be seen on Hugo's phone: docs/ios-app.md lists what to try. 外殼在沒有 Xcode 的環境撰寫：GitHub Actions 每次變更以 Xcode 26 與 27 編譯；網頁側有單元測試與端對端檢查。簽署、安裝與玻璃的實際樣子只能在 Hugo 的手機確認，清單見 docs/ios-app.md。
   - Weekly install 每週重裝 (step 2, 2026-11-14; ios/install.sh): one command, or a double-click on ios/重新安裝.command (it can sit in the Dock), does it all: brings the folder up to date when it is on main and unchanged, finds the iPhone (over the cable, or over Wi-Fi once Xcode is told to connect via network), builds and signs with the Apple ID Xcode holds (making the profile anew and registering the iPhone again when its 7 days are up), installs over the app so the sign-in stays, opens it, and says the date this build runs out. It builds in a folder of its own, so a signing file Xcode remembers but no longer has cannot stop it ("Build input file cannot be found … .mobileprovision", met by Hugo on 2026-11-14 when building in Xcode; the script starts that folder afresh once if it is met even so). Each failure it knows (no Apple ID in Xcode, an identifier taken, the free account's limits, Developer Mode off, a locked phone, an untrusted developer) is answered with what to do, in Chinese. Not unattended: the iPhone must be at hand and unlocked, and Xcode's sign-in to the Apple ID does lapse now and then. GitHub Actions runs both scripts with the bash a Mac has (3.2): ios/tests/scripts.sh. 一個指令或雙擊「重新安裝.command」（可放在 Dock）完成：在 main 且未改動時先更新程式、找到 iPhone（傳輸線，或在 Xcode 設定後用 Wi‑Fi）、用 Xcode 裡的 Apple ID 建置簽署（7 天到期的描述檔與裝置註冊會重做）、覆蓋安裝以保留登入、開啟 App，並顯示這一版的到期時間。使用自己的建置資料夾，不受 Xcode 記住的舊簽署檔影響（Hugo 於 2026-11-14 在 Xcode 遇到的錯誤）。已知的失敗各有中文說明。無法無人值守：手機要在旁邊並解鎖，Xcode 的 Apple ID 登入偶爾會過期。GitHub Actions 以 Mac 內建的 bash 3.2 檢查兩支腳本。
   - Reminders on the phone 本機提醒 (step 3, 2026-11-14; lib/models/local-reminders.ts, app/shell/reminders, components/shell-reminders.tsx, ios/Shell/Reminders.swift): what can be known ahead is scheduled by the phone itself, for the next 14 days, each time the app opens: the weekly review at its time, an event's reminder lead_days before it and a dividend on its asking day, both in the morning as the quiet hours end (09:00 without them), each in the push's own words and opening the push's page; and, from the shell itself, 20:00 on the evening before this build runs out (the date read from the provisioning profile inside the app). Nothing is scheduled inside the quiet hours. What cannot be known ahead (a rule warning, the volatility alert, news) stays with the web app's pushes. The choices are kept on the phone, on the push page inside the shell: the reinstall reminder on, the other three on only while no device gets the web app's pushes, so nothing comes twice. iOS asks once whether the app may notify. 可預知的提醒由手機自己排，每次開啟 App 排入未來 14 天：每週檢視在其時間；事件前（提前 lead_days 天）與配息日在早上安靜時段結束時（無安靜時段則 09:00）；文字與點開的頁面同推播；外殼自己再加「到期前一晚 20:00」的重裝提醒（日期讀自 App 內的描述檔）。不在安靜時段內提醒。無法預知的（規則警示、波動警報、新聞）仍由網頁版推播。選擇存在手機上（外殼內的推播頁）：重裝提醒預設開；其他三項只在沒有裝置收網頁推播時預設開，以免收到兩次。
   - From a push into the shell 從推播進入外殼 (step 3): a tapped push of the web app opens its page with `via=push` in the address (public/sw.js); on a device where Hugo has ticked "open pushes in the iOS app" (the web app's push page, off by default), the page asks iOS to open the same page in the shell through `longview://open?path=…`, a scheme the shell claims (free signing allows it; universal links would need the paid programme).
